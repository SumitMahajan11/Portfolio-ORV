Biyoo calm pose <img> tag in HTML:
<img src="/art/biyoo/biyoo-calm-360.webp" alt="Biyoo, a fluffy spherical cotton mascot with two striped amber horns floating in a calm, cheerful pose" width="100" height="89" decoding="sync" loading="eager" fetchPriority="high" class="biyoo-pose-calm absolute inset-0 w-full h-full object-contain pointer-events-none select-none" style="opacity:1"/>

Root-cause issues identified in server HTML:
1. The preload <link> lacked fetchPriority="high", causing Chrome to dispatch the initial request at priority "Low" (flagged by Lighthouse lcp-discovery-insight: priorityHinted = false).
2. The preload <link> used href and media rather than matching imageSrcSet and imageSizes to dedupe with the <picture> source.
3. The calm pose <img> had decoding="sync" instead of decoding="async".
