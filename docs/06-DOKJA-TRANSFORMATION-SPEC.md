# 06 Dokja Transformation Spec

Character: original-art Dokja, white coat, front-facing the entire sequence, no turning, no back view, no upward gaze. Horns red-black with thin red rim light. Wings white, layered feathers, wide enough to cross screen edges. Lit figure with cold rim light.

## Trigger split (provisional default)
Stages 1-3 auto-play on arrival (about 6-8s). Stages 4-7 are scroll-scrubbed (swipe on mobile) so the visitor provokes him. Scrolling back reverses stages 4-6.

## Stages
1. **Reading**: stands steady, reading the phone (the novel is this site). Only micro-life: coat edge, hair. Phone glow lights his face. Biyoo sleeps near the cursor.
2. **Notices you**: eyes lift from the phone to the viewer. Phone lowers slightly. Tiny system text: "A Reader has been detected."
3. **Irritation**: brow tightens, small sigh, phone gripped. Cold glow pulses. A scroll hint pulses at bottom.
4. **Horns grow** (scroll 0-25%): dark red-black horns emerge with red rim light. First and only red on the site.
5. **Wings emerge** (25-55%): white wings unfold from behind, slow and heavy.
6. **Wings expand** (55-85%): wings pass screen edges, star ring of tiny Constellation lights orbits him.
7. **Final form** (85-100%): glitch text "A Scenario has arrived." Biyoo wakes with a squeak. Page grid forms from drifting feathers (wing shatter into white squares) and the Scenario Select appears.

## Idle fallback
If no scroll after 8 seconds, Biyoo wakes, yawns, nudges the screen: "Scroll, reader."

## Finale (end of site)
Final form stands front-facing, closes the phone, looks at the reader, horns pulse red once, contact form appears. Optional camera button "Let the story see you?" Denied means no change.

## Layers per stage (see 08)
background, stars, wings-back, body, coat, horns, wings-front, phone, glow, particles. Animate transforms/opacity only; wings as 2 to 3 split layers with rotation origin at shoulders.

## Variants
- Mobile: portrait crop, wings spread vertical-swipe driven, particles 40.
- Light mode: wings as 1 layer scale/opacity, 15 particles.
- Reduced motion: 3 static frames (stage 1, stage 5, stage 7) crossfading on scroll.
